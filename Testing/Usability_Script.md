# Eight-step keyboard and spoken-label script

Baseline: NFR-03, TC-022, M12. Prerequisites: fresh F1, accepted supported MC, keyboard, configured Windows screen reader, recording consent when a participant is involved. No participant session has occurred yet.

Run three reset trials. Use Tab/Shift+Tab/Enter/Space and the screen reader; do not use a mouse.

1. Open/select CA, identify its spoken name and confirm visible focus.
2. Enter the supplied NOTE text in capture, identifying its label.
3. Save and hear/read the success result without losing the note.
4. Open the accepted MC practice question and identify its label.
5. Reveal the accepted answer and confirm its spoken label/content.
6. Follow its evidence link and identify the exact note/page and source text.
7. Return to practice; focus must be restored to the invoking item/control.
8. Rate recall and identify the resulting next-due status.

For each action record trial, step, completed without mouse (yes/no), spoken label, focus location, trap/error and evidence path. Target: 24/24 actions completed, zero keyboard traps and meaningful labels in every step. Record time and actual stakeholder comments as observations; do not invent a time threshold or user feedback that the requirements did not provide.
