## Purpose and scope

I am developing a local study assistant to help students keep notes and make study materials on their own computer for free. The current scope and proposed staged release are documented in Project-Description.md, the Design_Diagrams folder, and the Planning folder; advisor agreement on the release scope is pending.

## Responsibility and effort

I am the sole team member and primary owner of every task. My stated implementation availability is 4-8 hours per week; the plan uses a proposed 6-hour average with explicit contingency. I will record actual task hours, decisions, completed work and blockers in my design notebook each week.

## Communication and meetings

After an advisor agrees to serve for both semesters, I will arrange meetings at least once every two weeks at mutually agreed times and send a short progress/blocker summary beforehand. Meeting dates and decisions will be recorded. The advisor's contact details and meeting schedule are not yet confirmed.

## Decisions, verification and changes

I will keep project sources, design documents and task status in the team repository, review changes before submission, and record verification evidence separately from proposed behavior. I will document scope or schedule changes and discuss major changes with my advisor. If capacity or a dependency threatens a milestone, I will raise it promptly through the course/advisor process and update the plan rather than mark unfinished work complete.

## Working standards

I will preserve student originals, use permitted test materials, retain project/dependency notices, and review AI-assisted drafts under course rules. If another student joins, ownership, workload and conflict-resolution terms will be revised with that member and the advisor before work is reassigned.
